Belt test

The journey of a black belt begins with a white belt.

Every belt is earned, never given. Gradings test technique, kata, fitness and spirit in front of Shihan Raja.

  • TechniqueKihon done with power and form
  • KataForms for your grade, from memory
  • FitnessPush-ups, squats and endurance
  • SpiritAttitude, attendance and respect

More than belts,
it's a better you.

極真会
Belt 1White beltBeginner

The start of the journey. Etiquette, basic stances, punches and blocks.

Belt 2Yellow belt3 months

Basic kicks, first kata and the Japanese counting system.

Belt 3Orange belt3 months

Stronger stances, combinations and Taikyoku kata.

Belt 4Green belt4 months

Kumite fundamentals, Pinan kata and improved conditioning.

Belt 5Blue belt4 months

Controlled sparring, Sanchin breathing and timing.

Belt 6Brown belt6–12 months

Senior grade: teaching juniors, full kumite and advanced kata.

Belt 7Black belt1st Dan

Mastery of the basics. The real journey begins.

Dan 2Black belt2nd Dan

Deeper technique, strong kumite and a growing role teaching others.

Dan 3Black belt3rd Dan

Senior level: leads classes, mentors instructors and takes part in examining.
