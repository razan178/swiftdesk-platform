Questions

Kyokushin FAQ

Do I need any experience to join?

No. Most students start as complete beginners. Your first classes focus on basics, stances and fitness at your own pace.

Is Kyokushin safe for children?

Yes. Kids train without full contact, with close supervision, padding and age-appropriate drills that build coordination and confidence.

What should I wear to the free trial?

Comfortable sports clothes and a water bottle. You can buy a gi once you decide to join.

How much are the monthly fees?

It depends on how many days a week you train — 3, 4 or 6 days. See the full fee schedule on the Courses page.

How often are belt tests held?

Gradings are held regularly once a student has met the time and syllabus requirements for the next belt. See the Belt Test page.

Are there classes for women?

Yes. Self defense and fitness classes are open to women, and we can advise on the best class times.

Will karate help me lose weight?

Yes. Kyokushin training is high intensity. Our weight loss course combines karate conditioning with strength and circuit work.

Is the dojo affiliated?

Yes. Raja's Martial Arts & Fitness Gym is affiliated with I.K.O So-Kyokushin, and our instructors are certified and verified.
