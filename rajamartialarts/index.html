Programs

Find your class.

From a five-year-old's first white belt to adults chasing black — there's a place on the mat for you.

Ages 5–12

Little Warriors

Kyokushin fundamentals through structured, energetic classes. Kids build focus, coordination, manners and the confidence to stand up for themselves.

  • Belt grading system
  • Anti-bullying & safety skills
  • Fitness through play and drills
Ages 13–17

Teen Kyokushin

Sharper technique, controlled sparring and serious conditioning. Channel energy into discipline and earn every belt on the way up.

  • Kihon, kata & kumite
  • Tournament preparation
  • Strength & stamina work
Adults 18+

Adult Kyokushin

Traditional full-contact karate for complete beginners and experienced fighters alike. Train hard, learn real self-defence and get into the best shape of your life.

  • Beginner-friendly start
  • Full-contact sparring (optional)
  • Grading to black belt
All levels

Fitness & Conditioning

High-intensity strength and conditioning sessions built on martial-arts training. Burn fat, build power and improve stamina, with or without a gi.

  • Circuit & bag work
  • Strength and mobility
  • Coach-led every session

The path

White to black.

Progress you can see. Students grade through the Kyokushin kyu ranks — each belt earned through technique, kata, fitness and spirit.

  3. Blue
  4. Yellow
  5. Green
  6. Brown
  7. Black
