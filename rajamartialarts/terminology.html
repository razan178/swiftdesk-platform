Learn / Terminology

Kyokushin Techniques & Terminology

Explore the fundamental stances, blocks, strikes, kicks and kata of Kyokushin Karate. Learn the correct Japanese terms and the purpose behind each technique.

極真会

Stances立ち方 · Dachi

01 / 06

  • Zenkutsu-dachi

    前屈立ち

    Forward stance. Front knee bent, back leg straight, weight about 60% on the front leg.

  • Kiba-dachi

    騎馬立ち

    Horse-riding stance. Feet wide and parallel, knees pushed out, back straight.

  • Sanchin-dachi

    三戦立ち

    Hourglass stance. Knees pressed in, hips tucked — the root of Sanchin kata.

  • Kokutsu-dachi

    後屈立ち

    Back stance. Most of the weight on the back leg, ready to block and counter.

  • Fudo-dachi

    不動立ち

    Ready stance used in kumite and to begin many exercises.

  • Musubi-dachi

    結び立ち

    Formal attention stance. Heels together, toes apart — used when bowing.

Blocks受け · Uke

01 / 05

  • Jodan-uke

    上段受け

    Rising block that protects the head by sweeping the forearm upward.

  • Chudan soto-uke

    中段外受け

    Middle block from outside to inside, protecting the chest.

  • Chudan uchi-uke

    中段内受け

    Middle block from inside to outside.

  • Gedan-barai

    下段払い

    Downward sweeping block against low punches and kicks.

  • Mae mawashi-uke

    前回し受け

    Circular two-handed block, signature of Kyokushin.

Hand Strikes突き · Tsuki

01 / 06

  • Seiken chudan-tsuki

    正拳中段突き

    Straight punch to the body with the first two knuckles.

  • Oi-zuki

    追い突き

    Lunge punch, stepping forward with the punching side.

  • Gyaku-zuki

    逆突き

    Reverse punch from the back hand, driven by the hips.

  • Shita-tsuki

    下突き

    Upward punch to the ribs or stomach — a Kyokushin favourite.

  • Uraken ganmen-uchi

    裏拳顔面打ち

    Back-fist strike to the face.

  • Tettsui

    鉄槌

    Hammer-fist strike with the bottom of the fist.

Kicks蹴り · Geri

01 / 06

  • Mae-geri

    前蹴り

    Front kick with the ball of the foot.

  • Mawashi-geri

    回し蹴り

    Roundhouse kick with the shin or instep.

  • Yoko-geri

    横蹴り

    Side kick with the edge of the foot.

  • Ushiro-geri

    後ろ蹴り

    Back kick driving the heel behind you.

  • Hiza-geri

    膝蹴り

    Knee strike, close range.

  • Kakato-otoshi

    踵落とし

    Axe kick bringing the heel down from above.

Kata型 · Forms

01 / 05

  • Taikyoku Sono Ichi

    太極その一

    First kata learned — blocks and punches on an H-shaped pattern.

  • Pinan Sono Ichi

    平安その一

    The Pinan series builds flow, balance and combinations.

  • Sanchin

    三戦

    Breathing and tension kata that builds power and focus.

  • Tsuki no Kata

    突きの型

    A kata of punches, training speed and accuracy.

  • Gekisai Dai

    撃砕大

    Advanced kata of powerful, decisive techniques.

Combinations連続技 · Renzoku waza

01 / 04

  • Ichi-ni (jodan – chudan)

    上段・中段

    Two punches: one to the head, one to the body.

  • Gyaku-zuki – Mawashi-geri

    逆突き・回し蹴り

    Reverse punch followed by a roundhouse kick.

  • Shita-tsuki – Gedan mawashi

    下突き・下段回し

    Body punch into a low kick — a kumite classic.

  • Uke – Gyaku-zuki

    受け・逆突き

    Block and counter in one movement.
