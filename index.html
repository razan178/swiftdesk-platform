IELTS or PTE? Pick the test that fits your goal.

Both are accepted for study, work and immigration in most destinations — but one may suit your strengths and timeline better than the other. Here's the honest, simple version.

Test 1
IELTS

The International English Language Testing System — one of the most widely recognised English tests worldwide. Available in Academic and General Training versions.

  • Widely accepted for study, work & migration
  • Face-to-face or computer-based speaking
  • Often preferred by those who like a human examiner

May suit you if you're comfortable speaking with a person and want a test that's recognised almost everywhere.

Test 2
PTE

The Pearson Test of English — a fully computer-based test known for its automated scoring and typically fast results turnaround.

  • 100% computer-based, including speaking
  • Accepted for many study & visa applications
  • Often chosen when results are needed quickly

May suit you if you prefer speaking to a computer and want a fast, tech-driven format.

Still unsure which one is right for you? That's exactly what a two-minute conversation is for.
